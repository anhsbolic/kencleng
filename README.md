# Kencleng

This branch contains the approved upstream product seed for a fresh delivery-planning exercise. It does not define a release goal, delivery slices, engineering requirements, or an implementation baseline.

## Authority

- [Product intent](docs/product/product-intent.md) owns the current whole-product direction, actors, and trust commitments.
- [Product design standards](docs/ui-ux/README.md) own reusable design and interaction guidance, not product scope or business rules.
- Release scope, delivery order, capability requirements, and technical contracts remain open until explicitly decided and recorded in their appropriate future artifacts.

Illustrative examples in design material do not establish a feature commitment or a completed product state. Resolve any conflict in favor of the product intent for product meaning; ask the Human decision-maker when the product intent itself is insufficient.
