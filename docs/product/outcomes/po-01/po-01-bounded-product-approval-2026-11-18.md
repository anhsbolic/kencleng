## 2. Binding Product semantics approved in scope

The approved Product meaning is the behavior and constraints expressed by **§§1–8** of the immutable source, subject to its own limitations and the exclusions below; **§9 open issues and §10 readiness boundaries** govern the conditions of acceptance.

- **Product outcome and actor distinctions:** initial application/eligibility for legally recognized Indonesian Yayasan and Perkumpulan; account/Applicant/application/claimed evidence is not organizational authority; one recognized organization identity per underlying legal entity; no first-filer takeover or unsupported rival veto.
- **Stage and gate behavior:** draft and formal submission have different minimum thresholds; effective Approved requires the three substantive gates (legal entity and applicable eligibility, valid organization OA acceptance, valid First Owner mandate/acceptance) plus authorized human decision. Approval does not pre-certify legal sufficiency for any actual entity or mandate.
- **Human-led assessment:** EB-01..05 are Reviewer-facing criteria and relevant evidence must be attributable to its decision-time context; no mandated machine document reading, per-document classification engine or universal KTP/full Anggaran Dasar upload. Reviewer and Decision-Maker are authorized, accountable human responsibilities with conflict-of-interest safeguards.
- **Organizational commitments:** OA-01..05 accepted as one identifiable versioned package by a party authorized for that act, distinct from Applicant submission, First Owner personal acceptance and Kencleng's decision. No blanket agreement to unknown future terms.
- **Outcomes and exception meaning:** Approved, Rejected, Closed Inconclusive and Withdrawn are distinct. Clarification and authority conflict are not automatic fraud/rejection; duplicate or existing-organization claims do not silently transfer rights. A material post-approval correction is receivable, traceable and available for authorized handling, but does **not automatically** change eligibility or rights.
- **First Owner minimum behavior:** with valid mandate and effective approval, view legitimate organization eligibility/OA context, submit relevant clarifications/corrections and receive material eligibility communications. No implied Campaign publication, donation/funds permission, unilateral Owner replacement or broad Staff management.
- **Privacy, public disclosure and accountability as Product boundaries:** eligibility status, evidence and material decision history are private by default, with later public disclosure requiring separate Product approval; minimal meaningful business audit includes the evidence actually relied upon when the decision was taken, without claiming indefinite raw-file retention.
- **SC-01..SC-06:** binding Product success/acceptance **targets**, NOT executed UX, legal, security, system or Engineering tests.

No generic consent, universal KTP/NIK or arbitrary retention period is authorized.
