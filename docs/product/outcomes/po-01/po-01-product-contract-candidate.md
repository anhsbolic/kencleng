## 1. Outcome, actors and boundaries

**Outcome:** A person related to an Indonesian nonprofit legal entity can initiate and submit an Organization Eligibility application. Authorized Kencleng humans assess relevant evidence and decide initial eligibility lawfully, accountably and without misleading status/rights claims.

**Working legal-entity scope:** Indonesian legally recognized Yayasan and Perkumpulan with their own legal personhood. No universal exclusion based on size, newness, popularity, sector, previous Campaign/impact history or beneficiaries being members, while keeping actual legal-entity qualification and Kencleng eligibility requirements.

**Actor outcomes:** Applicant can submit and understand a bounded case without auto-acquiring authority; the legitimate organization is protected from first-filer takeover; Reviewer exercises human judgment without a machine document parser; authorized Decision-Maker is accountable for initial outcome; a recognized First Owner receives only bounded authority; downstream visitors/donors receive no inflated verification or Campaign claims.

**Included:** draft/submit/review/initial resolution, legal identity and eligibility, mandates and first Owner recognition, baseline organizational OA acceptance, truthful decisions, minimum evidence/audit traceability, bounded clarification/withdrawal/closure.

**Excluded:** ongoing eligibility monitoring/suspension/revocation, later Owner/Staff delegation or replacement, a complete mandate-dispute service, Campaign/fundraising authorization, donation/payment/funds use, UX/UI designs, APIs, databases, architecture, and engineering execution.

## 2. Product invariants

1. **One recognized organization identity per legal entity**; multiple Applicants give neither first-filer authority nor automatic rival veto.
2. **Application is not authorization**; authenticated account, uploaded evidence or claimed position are not accepted legal fact or mandate.
3. **Act-specific mandate** for submitting information, binding the organization to OA, appointing First Owner and exercising First Owner powers.
4. **All three approval gates and a legitimate human decision** required for effective Approved.
5. **Organization Eligibility is not Campaign/fundraising/donation/fund-movement/real-world-impact approval.**
6. **Unknown/pending is valid**; unverifiable or conflicting evidence is not automatic ineligibility or fraud.
7. **Truthful, private-by-default eligibility disclosure**; initial organization eligibility status, reasons and evidence are **not automatically public**, and the system must not claim to interpret legal documents independently. Internal authorized use is distinct from public disclosure.
8. **Proportionate accountability and protected history**; consequential events reconstructable for a lawful purpose, not unlimited raw-evidence retention.

## 3. Stage thresholds, gates and rights

| Stage | Required Product behavior | Not implied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start draft** | Authenticated Kencleng account; claimed organization name/identity, needed Applicant contact and stated relationship. | Proven legal entity, mandate, Owner/Staff rights or already-verified documents. |
| **Formal submission** | Reviewable entity claim with relevant document/official legal reference, Applicant attribution and initial claimed representative/basis. | Completed verification, valid organizational OA acceptance or accepted First Owner role as universal submission prerequisites. |
| **Initial approval** | Authorized human Review/Decision-Maker concludes all applicable gates sufficiently evidenced, with attestation/evidence references. | Downstream Campaign, funds, unlimited organizational rights. |

**Gate A:** legal identity and nonprofit/legal qualification for the specific Yayasan/Perkumpulan, plus proportionate Kencleng criteria; no subjective popularity/size/impact requirement. **Gate B:** a sufficiently authorized organizational party accepts the identifiable versioned OA-01..05 package. **Gate C:** a valid appointed First Owner Candidate with an authenticated account explicitly accepts bounded role/mandate, with legitimate Kencleng human recognition.

**Minimum First Owner capabilities only after effective Approved, subject to valid mandate:** (1) view the recognized organization's eligibility decision, relevant context and accepted OA commitments; (2) submit eligible-organization clarifications/corrections on its behalf for legitimate follow-up, without silently changing the prior decision; (3) receive material eligibility communications as an authorized organization contact. These describe meaningful Product abilities, **not** specified UI screens, mandatory notifications/delivery guarantees or exclusive communication rights. No automatic Campaign creation/publication, donation handling, fund control, Staff/Owner administration or unrestricted access.

## 4. Human-led review and evidence

**Reviewer-facing material domains:** EB-01 organization legal identity; EB-02 nonprofit/legal and Kencleng eligibility; EB-03 authorized representative mandate; EB-04 First Owner mandate and personal acceptance; EB-05 organizational OA acceptance.

These are human review criteria, **not mandatory per-claim software status objects, five separate workflows, per-document findings, required content-parsing engines or universal upload checklists**. Reviewer assesses relevant evidence and attests to sufficient compliance with applicable criteria. Routine positive attestation may be concise with evidence references; uncertain, conflicting, exceptional, rejected or material disputed cases need adequate rationale. Evidence is source-neutral; do not universally demand complete Anggaran Dasar, KTP/NIK, NPWP, bank account details or repeated uploads.

**Yayasan/Perkumpulan difference:** apply the same act-specific-authority rule. Yayasan representation depends on governing law, Anggaran Dasar and current mandate; no universal Pembina approval for every OA or appointment. Perkumpulan representation depends on relevant AD/ART, organ decisions and mandate; no universal rapat-anggota approval for every OA or appointment. Actual authority is assessed by an authorized human; titles or registry snapshots alone may be inadequate. Case-specific legal sufficiency remains an open legal/domain validation.

**Evidence decision-time context:** Review/decision references must identify the material actually relied upon **when the judgment occurred**, not just today's version of a mutable external link. Keep enough attributable context to reconstruct material decisions lawfully and proportionately. This does **not** require retaining every raw document, taking universal snapshots, or a per-claim machine assessment; records must not imply lawfully deleted evidence can still be opened.

## 5. OA-01..05 and single organizational acceptance

| ID | Working commitment | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OA-01 — Truthful Information** | Supply truthful material information; correct material inaccuracies once known. | Not a promise that mistakes never happen. |
| **OA-02 — Valid Representation** | Organization acts and representative/First Owner appointments remain within lawful authority. | Applicant is not automatically authorized. |
| **OA-03 — Material Change Disclosure** | Notify Kencleng of known changes materially affecting eligibility or representative authority. | Not every minor administrative change. |
| **OA-04 — Proportionate Cooperation** | Cooperate reasonably with legitimate proportionate Kencleng clarification and oversight. | No unlimited access to unrelated documents or personal data. |
| **OA-05 — Continuing Accountability** | If running Campaign/fundraising through Kencleng, fulfill clearly applicable accountability requirements including after fundraising. | No Campaign permission or pre-acceptance of unknown future requirements. |

**Single package, separate meaningful acts:** organizational representative accepts one identifiable OA version for that legal entity; Applicant terms and submission do not substitute; the First Owner Candidate separately accepts their personal role; Kencleng Reviewer/Decision-Maker separately judges and decides. Acceptance may have external evidence. No mandatory five checkboxes, universal digital-signature technology or forced external signatory Kencleng account.

**Minimum traceability:** organization, accepting person/organ and capacity, applicable OA version, attributable act and timestamp, relevant evidence references, Reviewer attestation and effective authorized decision. Material change in applicable OA terms before approval requires appropriate version acceptance; later new commitments cannot silently inherit earlier acceptance. Post-approval OA amendment handling and specific acceptance enforceability need separate legal/Product consideration.

## 6. Outcomes, clarification and exception edges

| Situation | Meaning and permitted consequences |
| --- | --- |
| **Approved** | All gates sufficiently met with authorized human decision. Organization initial eligibility and bounded First Owner rights effective. |
| **Rejected** | Proven material criterion failure or sufficiently evidenced authorized organizational refusal of baseline OA; reasoned authorized decision, not automatic fraud or permanent ban. Unsupported Applicant assertion of refusal is not organizational refusal. |
| **Closed Inconclusive** | After reasonable chance for clarification, material fact/evidence/mandate still unresolved. Case ends without initial rights or any claim that the entity is unlawful or fraudulent. |
| **Withdrawn** | Legitimately entitled Applicant withdraws a still-unapproved application; no new eligibility/Owner rights, no silent erasure of authorized organizational actions or material history. |
| **Clarification Required** | Reviewer requests relevant missing information; application undecided and Applicant receives an access-appropriate next step. Not rejection. |
| **Material mandate conflict** | Hold only the affected authority decision, use appropriate independent authorized escalation; unsupported competing claim is not a veto. |

After **Approved**, Applicant withdrawal cannot revoke existing organizational eligibility or Owner rights. A legitimate First Owner's **material correction after approval** must be received, traced to its source/actor/time and made available for appropriate **authorized Kencleng handling**, even if the correction may call the earlier eligibility basis into question. Such a submission **never automatically suspends, revokes, restores or changes** the effective eligibility or Owner rights: any reassessment or consequential rights change belongs to the separately authorized ongoing lifecycle (outside PO-01). This does not require a new case-management system, automated risk scoring, or prescribed routing mechanism in PO-01. New claimant to an eligible entity goes to separate existing-org authority/access handling, never second initial eligibility recognition. New evidence may justify a legitimate new decision, not silent overwrite of an earlier one. No arbitrary Product auto-closure timer; fair notification/opportunity and proportionate closeout required, with operational specifics still open. Intentional material misrepresentation requires substantiated facts and correct attribution; an Applicant's wrongdoing is not automatically attributed to an uninvolved legal entity.

## 7. Minimal accountable business events and privacy

**Business audit must answer:** what happened/event; when; where in the Kencleng domain (application/organization/resource); who (including relevant authority/capacity); for whom/what; result/effective consequence; and material context, such as evidence **identifiable as relied upon at the decision time**, applicable OA version and reason for a consequential decision. Include meaningful submission, material evidence change, OA/First Owner acceptance, Reviewer attestation, decision, effective rights change, material correction or escalation. Later source changes or lawful deletion must not silently alter the represented historical basis or imply retained raw evidence still exists. Do **not** prescribe universal evidence snapshots, every page view, keystroke, document-reading action, entire raw file content or a technical database/log schema.

**Security events remain Engineering-owned** under current Kencleng authorization/privacy/security standards and relevant [OWASP Logging guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html). IP/device/user-agent are not universally required business audit fields; use only for justified security/legal purpose. Maintain integrity and authorized access proportionally.

**Minimum personal-data purposes:** Applicant attribution/contact and claimed relationship; organization legal identity and relevant references; representative and First Owner mandate, attribution and acceptance; material decision and audit references. Limit access by assigned case, legitimate capacity/relationship and purpose; no open cross-Applicant evidence access. **Organization Eligibility status/decision and underlying evidence are private by default**, accessible only to authorized parties with a real need. Even a true Approved result does **not** automatically generate public status, badge or visible profile. Any outward publication, claim wording, eligible timing or audience requires a separate explicit Product disclosure decision; internal authorized downstream use alone is not public disclosure.

**Distinct retention purposes:** application/draft, raw evidence, and material decision/audit records. Preserve only what lawful purposes justify; if evidence is lawfully deleted, retained references cannot be represented as still-accessible raw evidence. **OPEN:** legal basis per processing purpose, third-party notice, audience/recipients, data-subject rights, retention triggers/periods, deletion exceptions and case-specific lawful obligations. No blanket consent, indefinite evidence storage or arbitrary lifetime.

## 8. Observable success and example acceptance probes

**SC-01:** legitimate application without organizational rights grant. **SC-02:** human-led review attestation without automated document understanding. **SC-03:** all gates + authorized human decision before approval. **SC-04:** Approved/Rejected/Closed Inconclusive/Withdrawn differ truthfully. **SC-05:** material decision traceable through minimal audit. **SC-06:** no unauthorized Owner, Campaign/funds rights, privacy breach or unsupported verification claim.

**Representative acceptance probes, not executed tests:** draft without mandate; reviewable but unverified submission; partial positive finding with OA/First Owner gate missing; full authorized approval; legitimate First Owner views eligibility/OA, submits authorized clarification/correction and receives material eligibility communications; **a post-approval material correction is traced and available for authorized Kencleng follow-up but does not automatically change status/rights**; unrelated Applicant cannot access those rights; initial status not publicly viewable by default; Reviewer conflict of interest; duplicate Applicants with unsupported vs credible competing mandates; claimant to already eligible organization; evidence uncertainty and fair inconclusive closure vs proven negative; withdrawal before vs after approval; OA version change; evidence changed/deleted after a decision without losing honest decision-time context or falsely promising original access; attempted Campaign/fund authority from org approval.

These are Product success targets and candidate scenario coverage, **not** evidence of system performance, completed tests, independently signed-off UX, legal clearance or implementation.
