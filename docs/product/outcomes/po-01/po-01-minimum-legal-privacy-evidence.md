## 2. Who can bind the organization? (authority is act-specific)

**SOURCE-CHECKED — Yayasan:** UU 16/2001 Pasal 35(1) gives Pengurus representative authority in and out of court, subject to limitations. Pasal 36 excludes representation in relevant conflict situations; Pasal 37(2) allows the Anggaran Dasar to limit Pengurus authority. UU 28/2004 changes Pasal 32–34 concerning appointment/change of Pengurus. **The title 'Ketua' or 'Pengurus' alone is not universal proof of authority for every commitment and delegation.**

**SOURCE-CHECKED — Perkumpulan:** Permenkum 18/2025 requires formation data/anggaran dasar that identify organs and governance-related matters (Pasal 12–13); changes to organs/anggaran dasar may be significant under Pasal 17. **This regulation alone does not establish that any person calling themselves chair can unilaterally accept all organizational obligations or appoint any First Owner.** Case-specific Anggaran Dasar/organ decision/mandate may be needed.

**PRODUCT IMPLICATION for EB-03/04/05:** Distinguish (a) the person who may legally bind the entity for OA-01..05, (b) actual authority to delegate a bounded Kencleng First Owner mandate, (c) candidate's explicit acceptance and account attribution. Source, current validity, decision/authority scope, limitations and contradictions all matter. A traceable mandate may be demonstrated by a relevant excerpt, organ decision or other sufficient lawful evidence; **full Anggaran Dasar is not universally mandatory**, but may be necessary in a particular case.

**OPEN / blocking for legal sufficiency and real-world reliance:** The exact legal sufficiency test for organization commitment acceptance, especially specific Perkumpulan governance variations, delegated authority and binding form of acceptance, requires appropriate review of an actual case. This source review is **not** lawyer verification. A separate, explicitly bounded and conditional approval of **Product semantics only** may be considered by Anhar without treating these legal facts as established; any affected enforceability or real-use claim remains blocked.

## 3. Minimum privacy evidence and boundaries

**SOURCE-CHECKED:** [UU 27/2022 — JDIH Kemkomdigi official text](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/832/t/undangundang%2Bnomor%2B27%2Btahun%2B2022), notably:
- **Pasal 16, 27–28:** processing includes collection, storage, access/disclosure and deletion; processing must be limited, specific, lawful, transparent and purpose-consistent.
- **Pasal 20:** controller must establish an applicable legal basis; **explicit consent is one of several available bases, not the universal sole basis**. Which basis validly applies to each PO-01 purpose is *not* settled here.
- **Pasal 21–24:** particular notice and proof requirements when processing relies on consent; other transparency and accountability obligations are not waived by choosing a different lawful basis.
- **Pasal 31 and 47:** recording/accountability of processing is required, but **does not grant indefinite permission to preserve every raw document**.
- **Pasal 42–45:** termination, deletion, destruction and notice are subject to statutory requirements; a request for deletion must be assessed legitimately, **not silently rejected for 'audit' or promised without checking lawful constraints**.

| Personal-data purpose (PO-01 candidate) | Minimum justified need | PRODUCT privacy boundary / OPEN |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Applicant account attribution** | Identify authenticated Kencleng user responsible for application action | Does not prove legal-person identity, position, or mandate; account data processing legal basis/notice and retention still to be mapped |
| **Authorized representative evidence** | Establish real person/organ and act-specific mandate for accepting obligations or delegating First Owner | Third-party name/signature/mandate may be processed only with a valid purpose + basis; Applicant's agreement cannot automatically stand in for all subjects; do not demand every individual's full ID by default |
| **First Owner acceptance** | Correct identity-to-account attribution, mandate scope, explicit and dated acceptance | Proportionate identity proof; no automatic full KTP/NIK/biometric collection |
| **Evidence review / initial decision** | Claim, provenance, actual examined facts, findings, authority and rationale | Preserve needed decision-time traceability under lawful retention; prefer minimum adequate information rather than indefinite document payloads |
| **Correction, dispute, data-subject request** | Legitimately reconstruct relevant decisions and handle requests | Retention/deletion and disclosure depend on role, purpose, lawful basis, legal hold/exception; no universal fixed period is derived from reviewed law |

**PRODUCT IMPLICATION:** Map purposes and authorized audiences separately for Applicant data, non-account-holding representatives, First Owner candidates, Reviewers, decision records and public claims. Organization/legal status does not justify publishing personal ID, private mandate evidence or other applicants' files.

**OPEN / blocking:** Determine for each actual personal-data purpose (including third-party mandates and sensitive evidence): lawful basis; controller/processor roles; necessary data and recipients; notice/human rights handling; retention trigger/duration, deletion and justified exceptions; any additional applicable legal obligations. Do not choose 'consent for everything', universal KTP, or an invented retention term.
