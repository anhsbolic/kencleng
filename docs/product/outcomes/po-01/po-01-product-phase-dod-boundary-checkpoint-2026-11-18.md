## 1. Harscode research fit — not a 27-item mandatory Harscode checklist

The [Harscode Product Definition — selected **research candidate**](https://github.com/anhsbolic/harscode-workspace/blob/research/cross-stage-mapping/product/product-definition.md) describes **four logical Product meaning groups** (O1 value intent, O2 legitimate Product commitments, O3 authority/constraints, O4 uncertainty/evidence), **not four mandatory files**, a fixed set of 27 checkboxes or a mandatory sequence. The [research Product Consumer Handoff](https://github.com/anhsbolic/harscode-workspace/blob/research/cross-stage-mapping/product/consumer-handoff.md) asks for **decision-specific sufficiency** for UX, UI and Engineering; it is not a requirement to produce their actual solutions. These Harscode sources are **research revision candidates**, not operational Harscode main policy or independent authority over Kencleng.

**The 27-item DoD is a local, inspectable PO-01 decomposition:** O1=4, O2=9, O3=4, O4=3, D=4, C=3. Of the 27, **24 concern local Product completion/consumer readiness** and **C.1–C.3 are local provenance checkpoints**. It is not 27 distinct new tasks. Existing decisions/evidence can support multiple unchecked criteria; an item is completed only after its **own evidence/accepted scope** is evaluated, not merely because text exists. A truly out-of-scope/non-blocking item may be explicitly justified under the owning §4 completion rule rather than treated as silently failed.

## 2. Product / UX / UI / Engineering boundary

| Question/obligation | Owning responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Which value/capability is promised, to whom; who may apply/accept/decide; which legal identity, data purposes, evidence/acceptance basis, rights/status changes, failure/unknown consequences must be true? | **Product**, with relevant **legal/privacy/domain** authority where material. Product cannot assert legal sufficiency without evidence. |
| Whether a review needs evidence proving legal entity status, actual representative authority or First Owner appointment; whether a source/reference can suffice; and at which stage information is necessary | **Product** specifies meaning and minimum requirements; legal/domain validates case-sensitive sufficiency. |
| How an Applicant/Reviewer discovers, understands, performs, interrupts and recovers; journey, interaction flow, IA, data-entry timing/layout, navigation, structural Lo-Fi | **UX**; Product scenario reasoning is allowed **only to check consequential semantic completeness**, not to pre-author UX flow. |
| Visual presentation and feature copy that honestly express accepted Product/UX meaning | **UI/Content**, with legal/Product approval for consequential claims and notices. |
| Actual upload/storage/API/authorization/log implementation, security/technical feasibility, verification/execution | **Engineering**, after appropriately authorized requirements, plus independently owned technical/security rules. |

**Prior “Focused Interaction Exploration” suggestion is not an obligatory Product phase task.** The proposed flows/Lo-Fi belong to **UX**. Product may use representative **domain scenarios** to test whether its commitments can be understood without inventing business semantics. Never add a separate Product journey artifact solely to satisfy this DoD.

## 3. Corrected D.1–D.4 consumer sufficiency interpretation

The earlier [assisted source-based Consumer Sufficiency Review](po-01-consumer-sufficiency-review-2026-11-18.md) remains historical evidence. This checkpoint **clarifies its overly broad “blocked/unverified” framing**, not the actual legal blockers:

- **D.1 UX / Experience Product input: CONDITIONAL.** Product meaning is sufficient for scoped UX inquiry/exploration, not evidence that UX has designed or validated a feature journey.
- **D.2 UI / Content Product input: CONDITIONAL.** Truthful internal state/claim boundaries can be consumed, but actual legally sensitive OA wording, privacy notices and final feature copy remain under owning authority.
- **D.3 Engineering Product information sufficiency: CONDITIONALLY CONSUMABLE.** Approved invariants/roles/outcomes can be inspected and challenged by Engineering **now** without inventing technical architecture. **Engineering implementation handoff is separately NOT READY/NOT AUTHORIZED**, with real-use legal/privacy and still-open consumer/behavior prerequisites. “Blocked for handoff” is **not** the same as “Engineering may not read/engage with Product”.
- **D.4 Actual Product-side sufficiency assessment: PERFORMED AS ASSISTED SOURCE REVIEW**, with D.1–D.3 evidence/limits recorded. **Independent receiving-team sign-off: NOT PERFORMED/UNVERIFIED**, but neither original PO-01 D.4 nor Harscode research makes it a universal prerequisite. D.4 is **left unchecked** until the Product owner judges the actual review evidence sufficient for the intended downstream decision.
- **None of D.1–D.4 is independently accepted/completed by this checkpoint.** Actual readiness is decision-specific; hold only affected legally dependent or implementation edges.

## 5. Standing and explicit non-decisions

It **does not decide** a mandatory document-upload list, legal adequacy of any particular Yayasan/Perkumpulan evidence or mandate, lawful privacy basis, retention period, final UX/Content flow, feature permissions, Engineering handoff or implementation.
