## 1. Agreed working behavior after checkpoint #2

1. **Competing applicants / one legal entity:** Duplicate applications are not automatically credible authority conflicts or vetoes. First-filer priority never establishes mandate. Assess materiality, credibility and relevance of each competing claim; only unresolved **credible material authority conflict** blocks the affected authority decision and triggers authorized independent escalation. No unwarranted cross-applicant evidence disclosure.
2. **Already eligible organization:** A new claimant does not start a separate initial approval producing a duplicate recognized organization identity; route the claim to legitimate existing-organization access/authority handling. The claim does **not** automatically transfer/withdraw Owner rights or suspend organizational eligibility. Credible material risk may require proportionate, independently authorized protective assessment; ongoing authority-management and suspension mechanics are outside PO-01.
3. **Minimum effective First Owner rights on approval:** Access the recognized organization context; learn applicable eligibility decision and obligations; represent the organization only for Kencleng actions specifically permitted within the valid mandate. No implicit Campaign publication, donations, fund handling, unlimited organizational powers, unilateral Owner replacement or Staff management.
4. **Eligibility vs fundraising permission:** Qualifying legal personhood and applicable Kencleng Organization Eligibility are distinct from authorization to undertake a specific Campaign or fundraising activity. A campaign-specific permit is not presumed to be a universal prerequisite for initial Organization Eligibility, nor may eligibility substitute for legally necessary fundraising permission. Formal legal validation remains pending.
5. **Kencleng inclusion policy:** Within the selected Yayasan/Perkumpulan legal-person scope, no universal exclusion by sector, beneficiaries being members rather than outsiders, organization age, size, popularity, website, prior Campaign history, or reported past impact. Eligibility still requires lawful nonprofit qualification, verifiable Kencleng criteria, valid organization accountability commitment, First Owner mandate/acceptance, and authorized manual decision.
6. **Material disqualification:** Proven material legal/policy ineligibility can support reasoned substantive rejection. Proven intentional material misrepresentation may warrant adverse decision only with appropriately evidenced conduct, responsibility, intent and relevance; Applicant wrongdoing is not automatically attributable to an uninvolved legal entity. Unsupported accusations, administrative corrections, unclear mandates or incomplete/unverifiable evidence are **not automatically substantive rejection or fraud**. Campaign-specific restrictions remain distinct.
7. **Claim-based, source-neutral evidence sufficiency:** Assess *facts/claims* EB-01 through EB-05, not document quantity. No universal full Anggaran Dasar upload, identity-card copies, or duplicate document collection if material facts can be established sufficiently by appropriate source evidence. Different legal-entity governance may require different proof of actual representative mandate. Official existence/status evidence does not automatically prove internal appointment/delegation authority.
8. **Traceable assessment:** Every material claim supporting an initial decision must link source/provenance, examination time and material validity, *what was checked*, finding and limitations, Reviewer attribution, applicable criteria, Decision-Maker authority/rationale, effective chronology and correction history. Submission, assessed claim and authorized decision are distinct. Reconstruct information relied upon **at the decision time**, not only a live link that may change; no universal screenshot/full-document retention rule.
9. **Purpose- and risk-based personal data:** Account authentication is not legal identity or organizational mandate. Request extra identity, representative or third-party personal data only where material and proportionate to valid attribution, mandate, acceptance or review. No universal NIK/KTP, personal NPWP, personal banking data, full ID scan, or mandatory generic sensitive-data upload. Respect bounded audience-specific disclosure; Applicant terms/consent alone do not automatically authorize processing third-party data.
10. **Purpose-based retention:** Keep material decision rationale/evidence provenance sufficiently traceable for legally justified accountability, while avoiding perpetual raw-evidence retention. Preservation/deletion periods, lawful bases, third-party privacy, erasure exceptions, and dispute/legal holds require purpose-specific privacy/legal validation. Withdrawal, rejection or closure do not authorize silent rewriting of decisions; neither does auditable history justify unlimited retention.

## 4. Later working clarification — minimal human-led review and audit (18 November 2026)

> **Human decision:** Anhar explicitly agreed to simplifying overly detailed evidence assessment and requested a minimal, meaningful audit trail. This paragraph is a **later working decision**, and takes precedence over any interpretation of §1 items 7–8 above requiring structured system interpretation of every material claim, exhaustive per-document findings, or comprehensive business activity logs. Historical wording is retained to preserve how the decision evolved. Binding approval, legal sign-off and implementation authorization are still **not** granted.

- **Keep EB-01..05 as Reviewer guidance.** A suitably authorized person, not an automated legal-document engine, reads relevant evidence and attests whether the applicable requirements are met. Sufficient/insufficient/unverifiable/contradictory/disqualifying describe human assessment possibilities, **not mandatory field-level, file-level or machine-produced statuses**.
- **Minimal attestation:** the human reviewer's conclusion about eligibility criteria, linked evidence references, attributable identity and time; material uncertainties, rejection, exceptions or conflict need a concise meaningful explanation. The Decision-Maker's authorized outcome and effective consequence must be reconstructable. Reviewer and Decision-Maker may be one non-conflicted authorized person.
- **Minimal consequential business audit:** for a relevant event, be able to answer **what/event, when, where in the Kencleng business context (application/organization/resource), who (and authorized capacity), for whom/what, outcome, and material context**. Evidence/terms/version references and rationale are included for decisions/attestations where needed; this is **not a mandatory universal audit schema**.
- **Log consequential events, not every interaction:** application submission, material evidence changes, organization/Owner acceptance, review attestation, initial decision, effective privilege/authority change, important correction/escalation or similar material actions. No default page-view logging, keystroke history, capture of every document-reading action, full document-content interpretation, or indefinite raw-document retention.
- **Separate OWASP-aligned security logging:** Security-relevant activity and technical evidence follow the effective Kencleng Engineering security/privacy standards; reducing business audit events **does not reduce necessary security logging**. IP/device/user-agent/technical location are not mandatory *business* audit fields and should only be captured for an established security/legal purpose. OWASP is reference guidance, not an assertion of audited compliance.
- **Source-of-current-meaning:** [PO-01 §5.3–5.4](po-01-organization-entry-initial-eligibility.md) carries the simplified latest working interpretation. Existing Actor & Authority Model traceability language is read as an **accountability outcome**, not compulsory per-claim technical decomposition.
