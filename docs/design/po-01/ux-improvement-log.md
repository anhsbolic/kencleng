# PO-01 — UX Improvement Log (Kencleng × Harscode)

> **Standing:** WORKING RESEARCH / IMPROVEMENT LOG — proposals and learning, **not** approved Product, UX, UI, Project Standard, or Harscode authority.
> **Scope:** UX exploration for PO-01 (Organization Entry & Initial Eligibility Decision), Kencleng `research/cross-stage-mapping`.
> **Started:** 20 November 2026, with Anhar's explicit approval to create this file in the Kencleng repository.
> **Purpose:** Keep material opportunities to improve **Kencleng** and/or **Harscode** reconstructable across sessions, without automatically amending either owning authority.

## Use and authority boundaries

- Record an improvement candidate **only when it has a material potential lesson beyond the immediate UX decision**. Do not mirror every chat message, UX artifact, or open Product question.
- Separate **KNOWN** authoritative facts, **OBSERVED** evidence, **INFERRED** analysis, **HYPOTHESIS**, and **UNKNOWN**; expert/agent critique is not actor testing or consumer acceptance.
- This log can recommend work for Kencleng or Harscode. **Kencleng Product/Design/feature UX** and **Harscode definitions** remain owned in their respective repositories and accepted exact revisions. Changes require appropriate owner review, Anhar's scoped approval where needed, and persistence in the **owning** source.
- **Working approach/DoD decisions belong in the appropriate PO-01 UX artifact** after scoped acceptance. This log tracks possible reusable improvements and later evidence, not a rival UX definition or DoD.
- Use source links, revision/approval standing, consumer impact, and next validation when material. Do not promote a candidate to project-wide guidance from one untested example.
- On promotion or rejection, retain the original finding and record the target source/revision and actual disposition; do not silently delete its history.

## Minimal entry information

For each material entry, capture: **finding → evidence/limits → affected consumer → potential target owner/source → proposed improvement or test → current disposition**. Add dates or exact revision links when the finding is tested or decided. Avoid mandated separate files or a heavyweight process.

Disposition language: **Candidate** (plausible, not yet validated), **Review recommended** (evidence justifies owner evaluation), **Promoted** (owning source changed with approval), or **Closed — no change** (existing guidance sufficient, disproven, or not worth changing). These are log labels, not product/runtime states.

## Current entries

### UX-IMP-01 — Copy-complete Indonesian experience proof

- **Finding / working decision (20 Nov 2026):** For material PO-01 interaction moments, UX Lo-Fi should include **mature, contextual, natural Indonesian actor-facing copy** sufficient to evaluate comprehension and generally reusable by UI/Content without substantive rewriting. UI retains final UI wording/copy ownership; zero UI changes is **not** a quality gate.
- **Evidence / limits:** **KNOWN:** Kencleng [Indonesian UX writing guidance](../copywriting-id.md) already says write from reader intent rather than translate literally, and evaluate Natural, Relevant, Truthful, Actionable, Consistent, Accessible. **KNOWN (selected research guidance):** Harscode [UX Definition](https://github.com/anhsbolic/harscode-workspace/blob/research/cross-stage-mapping/design/ux/definition.md) owns comprehension/interaction proof; [UI Definition](https://github.com/anhsbolic/harscode-workspace/blob/research/cross-stage-mapping/design/ui/definition.md) owns O7 Final UI Copywriting. **UNKNOWN:** whether any Kencleng/Harscode reusable standard actually needs changing. No tested PO-01 Lo-Fi or actor/consumer evaluation yet.
- **Consumer impact:** UI/Content should receive meaningful reusable copy, not be forced to invent communication semantics; Engineering should receive clear consequential states/actions. Exact legal/privacy and domain wording remains subject to its actual authority.
- **Target / next test:** Apply this expectation to the **proposed bounded PO-01 UX DoD** (not yet accepted) and evaluate representative Lo-Fi and actual receiving-UI comprehension. Only propose changes to `docs/design/copywriting-id.md` or the legitimate Harscode owner if a material repeatable gap is evidenced.
- **Disposition:** **Candidate — validate during PO-01 UX**. Not a proposal to update global standards now.

### UX-IMP-02 — Same page, different actor/context experience

- **Finding / working agreement (20 Nov 2026):** A shared conceptual page/surface may present materially different experiences by actor, actual authority/relationship, scenario, and state. UX should expose representative material variants, not one generic screenshot, while challenging whether a shared page is appropriate at all.
- **Evidence / limits:** **INFERRED** from PO-01 cross-actor eligibility/review consequences; no accepted PO-01 page inventory or tested screen proof. Current Harscode UX C2–C5 already covers branches, Lo-Fi, page-versus-state/navigation, and feedback; a standards gap has **not** been demonstrated.
- **Consumer impact:** UI/Content needs **page/surface-centric** visibility into material variants; Engineering and UX review need **scenario-centric** consequences and continuity. Avoid an exhaustive actor × page × status matrix.
- **Target / next test:** During PO-01 interaction exploration, derive (rather than presuppose) a page/surface inventory and inspectable variants. Evaluate consumer sufficiency before considering Harscode or Kencleng standards changes.
- **Disposition:** **Candidate — validate during PO-01 UX**.

### UX-IMP-03 — Scenario-centered, actor-grounded UX with continuity

- **Finding / agreed working approach (20 Nov 2026):** Use **scenarios** as the unit of exploration/proof and **actor-in-context** as the inquiry perspective. Inspect cross-actor dependencies and preserve each actor's journey/continuity across scenarios.
- **Evidence / limits:** **INFERRED** fit for PO-01 because Applicant, organizational representative, First Owner Candidate, Reviewer, and Decision-Maker have material authority dependencies. This is **not** a universal Harscode method decision; no observed consumer feedback has established superiority.
- **Consumer impact:** UI/Content must reconstruct the complete relevant actor experience; Engineering needs coherent cross-actor behavior/branch meaning without reverse-engineering separate journeys.
- **Target / next test:** Evaluate against real PO-01 scenario proofs and actor continuity, then assess whether the existing Harscode UX Definition already handles the need sufficiently.
- **Disposition:** **Candidate — validate during PO-01 UX**.
