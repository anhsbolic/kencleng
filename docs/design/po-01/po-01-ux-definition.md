## 2. Agreed UX approach and quality expectations

**Scenario-centered, actor-grounded** is the agreed **PO-01 working approach** (not a universal Harscode methodology): use representative human situations as the unit of exploration and proof; explicitly identify the focal actor at each interaction moment, their goal, knowledge, authority/context and affected parties; preserve **actor continuity across scenarios** and consistency of cross-actor consequences. Actors include Applicant, authorized organizational representative, First Owner Candidate, Reviewer/Decision-Maker and legitimately affected organization/parties; none becomes the default authority merely from starting an application.

**Page/surface reasoning:** Derive a **page/surface inventory from scenario and interaction exploration**; do not presuppose a one-page-per-actor architecture. For a shared surface, make materially different experiences inspectable by actor **plus actual authority/relationship, scenario and state**, including information hierarchy, available actions, feedback and continuity. Decide page-versus-in-page-state within UX authority; neither UI nor Engineering should have to infer it. Cover representative material combinations, **not** a universal actor × page × state matrix.

**Copy-complete UX proofs:** At the point a material Lo-Fi is proposed for UX acceptance, include **mature, natural, contextual Bahasa Indonesia** for materially consequential actor-facing headings, instructions, status explanations, actions, confirmation, uncertainty and recovery, as relevant. Start from reader intent and truthful Product meaning, **not English-to-Indonesian translation**. It must be good enough to evaluate comprehension and ordinarily **reusable by UI/Content without substantive rewriting**. UI/Content retains final wording and contextual polish; **zero copy edits is not a completion metric**. Do not prematurely polish throwaway concepts, use placeholder text that prevents material evaluation, invent exact legal/privacy text, or promote candidate copy to approved disclosure. Any wording change that alters UX or Product meaning returns to its legitimate owner.

### 7.2 Agreed working approach — meaning-complete Lo-Fi

On 22 Nov 2026, Anhar agreed these **working UX exploration refinements** beyond the previously persisted three §2 refinements; they are **not a blanket new cross-project Standard or an approval of any individual Lo-Fi**:

1. **Meaning-complete UX proof**, not copy-and-boxes-only: inspect material actor-facing copy, information hierarchy, *information sequence*, entry/exit and action/disclosure behavior, and meaningful visual communication intent where needed.
2. **Information priority + sequence + access:** ask what matters *now*, what must be known *before a later consequence*, what is genuinely useful *on demand*, and what should not be on this surface. The unit is **one meaningful understanding/decision focus per experience moment**, **not** a universal rule of one value per physical viewport. Scroll, navigation, action, accordion, or dedicated page are candidate mechanisms to choose proportionately. Preserve a discoverable direct path for already-ready people; never hide trust-critical facts to enforce visual storytelling.
3. **Visual/media intent:** UX describes why a visual matters, intended meaning, suggested media type (e.g., real documentary photography, editorial illustration, vector/explanatory diagram, animation), placement in the experience, non-negotiable truth constraints, and UI creative freedom. UX does **not** require an icon/image/animation on every surface or own final rendering, composition, licensing or production. Choose the medium based on comprehension needs; real photography used as factual context/evidence requires appropriate provenance/permission. Generated/synthetic imagery must not masquerade as real beneficiaries, organizations, Campaigns, evidence, verification or impact.
4. **UI boundary:** UI/Content owns final expression and final copy, and may propose media alternatives that preserve confirmed meaning. Material UX sequencing/action semantics and Product rights/truth remain with their respective owners. A motion candidate must not make information inaccessible without motion (e.g., reduced-motion/accessible static alternative).

### 8.1 Effective Product invariants and scope

This checkpoint uses the **exact bounded Product successor blob** and separate Human approval linked in §1. **Draft ≠ formal submission ≠ Approved.** Formal submission needs a reviewable legal-entity claim, relevant legal document/official reference, attributable Applicant and initial claimed representative/basis, **not** already-accepted mandate, OA and First Owner as universal submission conditions. Act-specific authority applies to different acts; first filer gets no automatic organization rights. Approval requires **Gate A** legitimate nonprofit/legal qualification, **Gate B** valid authorized acceptance of **one versioned OA-01..05 package** for the organization, **Gate C** valid appointment + authenticated explicit personal acceptance and legitimate recognition of First Owner, plus authorized human decision; the order of these conditions is **not a mandated UX sequence**. Unknown, pending and conflicts are not automatically fraud/rejection. **Approved** creates only bounded organization eligibility and first Owner rights; not Campaign/funds, unlimited access or public status/badge. Status/reasons/evidence remain private by default. Material correction after Approved is accepted/traced/available for authorized handling but **never automatically changes effective status or rights**.

### 8.3 Representative scenario scripts and candidate copy meaning

**R1 — B2/helper → reviewable submission → clarification:** visitor understands no proven mandate is needed to *start a draft*. At draft distinguish **claimed relationship** from **accepted representative authority**. At formal submission show legal-entity reference and initial claimed representative/basis, with clear attribution and confirmation: “Pengajuan formal berarti meminta Kencleng meninjau klaim organisasi berdasarkan informasi yang Anda sampaikan”; “Pengajuan bukan persetujuan kelayakan atau pengakuan kewenangan.” If reviewer asks, candidate Applicant response may be “Saya belum dapat memastikan informasi tersebut” without creating false certainty; “Klarifikasi diperlukan. Permohonan belum diputuskan—ini bukan penolakan.” **No real submit/response was executed.** Need inspect seamless return, context and actor capacity when joining prior First Encounter.

**R2 — Positive organizational path:** submitted claim → legitimate human review of legal qualification → **authorized organizational OA versioned acceptance** (may be external) → **valid appointed candidate's own authenticated acceptance** → authorized human recognition/approval → First Owner sees limited rights. This is **representative conceptual ordering only**; not universal stage sequence or invitation/account model. Representative copy for Applicant “Penerimaan kewajiban organisasi tidak otomatis memberi Anda kewenangan”; for candidate “Anda belum menjadi First Owner hanya karena nama Anda diusulkan”; after approval “Kelayakan awal organisasi telah disetujui” **paired immediately** with no automatic Campaign/fundraising/donation rights. **One person may occupy multiple capacities only with appropriate act-specific authority and separately attributable acts.**

**R3 — Outcome and historical integrity:** clarification required vs inconclusive closeout vs sufficiently evidenced rejected vs Applicant-initiated withdrawn; show **result → specific basis → applicable consequence → truthful available next action**. Example Inconclusive “Permohonan ditutup tanpa kesimpulan kelayakan” and no implication organization proved unlawful or fraudulent; Rejected “Permohonan ditolak karena kriteria material dinilai tidak terpenuhi” with actual reason; Withdrawn “Permohonan ditarik” only **after effective eligible withdrawal**, not on a button tap; Approved → First Owner material correction is **new attributed information** with historical decision basis preserved, not an edit of the prior Approved judgment. **No automated statuses or legal claims were validated.**

**Shared copy and comprehension constraints:**
- Use natural Indonesian action-specific copy at actual consequential moment; no generic “permohonan tidak berhasil” collapsing epistemically different outcomes.
- Place *needed-now* facts inline, *needed-before-later* consequences at relevant review/confirmation, secondary detail on demand; relevant legal/privacy text cannot be fabricated or hidden.
- “Bukti tersedia/diajukan” ≠ “telah dinilai valid”; “penerimaan pribadi ada” ≠ “penunjukan sah”; “Gate complete” ≠ “Approved”; “koreksi diterima” ≠ “keputusan berubah”.
- Status/decision reasons and raw evidence are **private by default** and access-appropriate, including competing Applicants; never claim any globally public eligibility profile/badge.
- Do **not** infer from the Lo-Fi: real persistence, authenticated rights, verified documents, formal acceptance, notification, receipt, campaign authorization, funding, fundraising outcome or automatic trust proof.

### 8.5 Cross-scenario risks, gaps and ownership

**High consequence; must be preserved in UX and consumer communication:**
1. **Authority continuity:** Applicant, org representative, candidate, Reviewer/Decision-Maker have different act-specific capacities; same person does not inherit a different authority automatically.
2. **Privacy/access:** never expose other Applicant, candidate or organization evidence/eligibility decision without legitimate need and authorized access. No public badge.
3. **Approval decision integrity:** Gate A/B/C are not clicks or universally linear screens; no rights until effective authorized human Approved decision.
4. **Historical integrity:** evidence judged at decision time remains distinguishable from later source changes/deletions or corrections. Never imply deleted raw evidence is still available.

**Open UX / experience decisions:** exact transitions from First Encounter/auth conceptual boundary into real draft; case-entry continuity after login; clear interrupted-work recovery; final page vs state and navigation context; how representative accesses OA context if acceptance occurs externally; how candidate finds valid appointment context without an invented invitation mechanism; neutral refusal/no-answer treatment; access-appropriate case-status and decision reasoning for Applicant; reasonable Applicant recovery after a decision; how corrections are received, acknowledged and followed up without unauthorized status mutation; copy-complete integrated screen variants; narrow-mobile, keyboard/focus and realistic responsive tests.

**Open Product decisions (do not solve by UI invention):** consequences of First Owner Candidate explicitly declining and nomination alternatives before approval; appeal/refiling/reopen rules and fair clarification time specifics; consequential known mandate-conflict disclosure obligations and exceptional escalation communications if they materially require new policy. Current binding minimums still apply while operational specifics remain open.

**Open legal/domain/privacy:** actual Yayasan/Perkumpulan act-specific representative/appointment legal sufficiency, OA enforceability, processing bases and notices for third parties, access/disclosure, retention/deletion and lawful evidence handling. **Blocks affected real-world reliance/processing; not permission to process real data in UX tests**.

**Open Engineering constraints (boundary only):** feasible safe authentication/authorization, privacy, document/link reference handling, event traceability, interruption/save/resume and cross-actor continuity. Do not decide API, schema, storage, invitation, document parser, automatic scoring or implementation in UX.
