## 2. Agreed UX approach and quality expectations

**Scenario-centered, actor-grounded** is the agreed **PO-01 working approach** (not a universal Harscode methodology): use representative human situations as the unit of exploration and proof; explicitly identify the focal actor at each interaction moment, their goal, knowledge, authority/context and affected parties; preserve **actor continuity across scenarios** and consistency of cross-actor consequences. Actors include Applicant, authorized organizational representative, First Owner Candidate, Reviewer/Decision-Maker and legitimately affected organization/parties; none becomes the default authority merely from starting an application.

**Page/surface reasoning:** Derive a **page/surface inventory from scenario and interaction exploration**; do not presuppose a one-page-per-actor architecture. For a shared surface, make materially different experiences inspectable by actor **plus actual authority/relationship, scenario and state**, including information hierarchy, available actions, feedback and continuity. Decide page-versus-in-page-state within UX authority; neither UI nor Engineering should have to infer it. Cover representative material combinations, **not** a universal actor × page × state matrix.

**Copy-complete UX proofs:** At the point a material Lo-Fi is proposed for UX acceptance, include **mature, natural, contextual Bahasa Indonesia** for materially consequential actor-facing headings, instructions, status explanations, actions, confirmation, uncertainty and recovery, as relevant. Start from reader intent and truthful Product meaning, **not English-to-Indonesian translation**. It must be good enough to evaluate comprehension and ordinarily **reusable by UI/Content without substantive rewriting**. UI/Content retains final wording and contextual polish; **zero copy edits is not a completion metric**. Do not prematurely polish throwaway concepts, use placeholder text that prevents material evaluation, invent exact legal/privacy text, or promote candidate copy to approved disclosure. Any wording change that alters UX or Product meaning returns to its legitimate owner.

**Three explicitly agreed refinements now integrated:** (1) scenario-centered/actor-grounded with cross-scenario actor continuity; (2) derived page/surface inventory plus material shared-page experience variants; (3) copy-complete Indonesian Lo-Fi and language quality evaluation, designed for strong UI reuse while UI owns final wording.

**Representative probes for planning, not a fixed screen list or already executed tests:** first-time Yayasan Applicant start and formal submit; clarification and distinct negative/inconclusive/withdrawal meanings; legitimate Reviewer review, conflict/competing mandate handling; authorized OA acceptance and First Owner Candidate's separate personal acceptance; bounded Approved/First Owner rights and later material correction without automatic status change; Perkumpulan differences where material. Coverage is selected based on consequence, not every Product exception mapped to a screen.

**DoD disposition guidance when evidence exists:** **SUPPORTED** (actual source/review/proof sufficient for bounded decision), **CONDITIONAL** (explicit limits and non-blocking conditions), **BLOCKED** (material affected decision lacks legitimate meaning/approval/evidence), **UNVERIFIED** (not inspected). This is evaluation vocabulary, not automatic global stage status. A material UX acceptance requires actual inspectable proof and legitimate Human disposition; approved criteria alone are never a UX-complete declaration.

## 4. Non-negotiable open dependencies for PO-01

- Actual Yayasan/Perkumpulan legal identity/mandate and organizational OA enforceability examples remain subject to **qualified legal/domain review**; UX cannot certify them.
- Real personal-data processing bases/notices, third-party processing, retention and deletion specifics remain **unvalidated for real use**. UX may safely explore using **fictional/synthetic** cases, with explicit simulation standing.
- Fair clarification windows, appeal/refiling and exceptional conflict details are not a license to invent timers, guaranteed routes or permissions. Escalate material missing Product meaning when a proposed experience genuinely depends on it.
- **Draft ≠ submitted ≠ Approved.** No first-filer Owner rights. Gate A/B/C and legitimate authorized human decision precede Approved; no Campaign, funds or public visibility follows automatically.
- Harscode research definitions and these local criteria do not confer legal/privacy, UI/Hi-Fi, Engineering or deployment approval.

## 5. O1 inquiry starting point — WORKING / NOT YET VALIDATED

**Selected first exploration context:** first-time **Yayasan Applicant** approaching initial organization eligibility. This is **one starting scenario**, not adoption of Applicant as the sole UX PoV or approval of any page, invitation channel or form flow.

**KNOWN — accepted Product input:** An authenticated Kencleng account may start a draft with claimed organization identity, Applicant contact and stated relationship, without proven organizational mandate. A **formal submission** requires a reviewable claim with relevant document/official reference, attribution and initial representative claim/basis. Neither moment grants Owner power or ensures eligibility. Approval requires later Gate A/B/C and authorized human decision, while real privacy/legal clearance is not established. (Source: current approved successor and separate approval above.)

**HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN — actor experience, not Product fact:**
- **H1:** Applicant may arrive expecting to “mendaftarkan Yayasan”, and may not initially distinguish **mengajukan permohonan** from **Yayasan disetujui**. Neither actual terminology nor misunderstanding has been observed.
- **H2:** A first-time Applicant may be unsure whether they must already have formal authority or complete legal paperwork before taking the first step. This may affect trust and perceived effort; no direct actor observation yet.
- **H3:** The person initiating may **not** be the person who can legitimately accept organizational accountability or serve as First Owner. Product makes the distinction; the lived division of work and expected handoff remain unknown.
- **UNKNOWN:** Concrete discovery touchpoint, first-visit motivation, digital context/accessibility needs, familiarity with Kencleng's eligibility concept, willingness to provide third-party information and actual language comprehension. Do not invent landing pages, save/reopen behavior, invites, alerts or account mechanisms.

**O1 human problem candidate (INFERRED, not validated):** How can a person who believes they are helping a Yayasan approach Kencleng, understand whether it is worth beginning an eligibility application, know what is expected **now versus later**, and take a legitimate first step **without mistaking a draft/application for organizational recognition or claiming authority they do not yet have**?

**Priority inquiry before committing interaction design:** Investigate the actor's **entry intention and expectation of consequence**: what do they think “mendaftarkan organisasi” accomplishes, whose authority do they believe they represent, and what information would make starting a truthful, proportionate choice? Challenge with contrast scenarios: the actor is just an administrative helper; the actor is actually authorized; they have partial legal evidence; an already-recognized organization or rival claim exists. These are **inquiry probes**, not approved behaviors or mandatory screens.

**Next UX activity:** P1–P2 deepen actor-in-context framing, evaluate meaningful alternatives for the first-contact experience, identify high-value evidence and genuine Product gaps, then proceed iteratively to experience concept and inspectable interactions when justified. No page inventory, Lo-Fi, actor comprehension claim or acceptance is established in this section.
