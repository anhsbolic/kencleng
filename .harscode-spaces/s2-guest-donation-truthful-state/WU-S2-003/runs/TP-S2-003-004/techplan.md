## 1. Background

Slice 2 requires a guest Donation whose persisted sandbox outcome and Campaign funding remain truthful. At the prior Draft, the exact campaign funding-capacity policy and its API/data shapes were unresolved. WU-S2-006 has since accepted the exact Campaign/Donation spec and authored split API source revisions, and its Build reconciled the generated API, frontend types, public fixtures, and focused fixture correspondence test. ## 4. Rules & Validation

- **R1 — Scope and guest path:** Implement only accepted guest submit/status, simulator, required opted-in notification, minimum Campaign D1 integration, and accepted availability/cap projection. Account, donor list/history/claim, real rails, and broader Slice 3 remain absent.
- **R2 — Exact amount and per-Campaign cap:** Accept whole IDR from Rp5.000 through the effective Campaign cap, including Rp5.001; reject below-minimum, fractional, invalid, or over-cap input. Default and preserve cap values per create/PATCH rules, freeze after publication, and retain exact decimal representation end-to-end.
- **R3 — Public cap wire shape:** Every Campaign response and public detail serialize `max_donation_amount` as exactly `{amount, currency_code}` with required decimal-string amount and IDR. Public detail retains it when Funding is unavailable. The typed DTO/mapping and exact-wire key/member assertion must match the accepted generated contract.
- **R4 — Request idempotency ordering:** Under the selected Campaign-first transaction boundary, resolve an existing key and compare canonical payload before new-intent eligibility rejection. Same key/payload returns the original Donation/result after close; changed payload rejects; absent key proceeds to current atomic eligibility/admission. One accepted intent creates one Donation. Settlement replay remains separately idempotent.
- **R5 — Capacity admission and errors:** Count collected settled Funding plus full accepted-pending obligations. Never admit above IDR 99,999,999,999,999,999. Close with `funding_capacity_reached` if residual capacity is under Rp5.000, including zero (Rp4.999 closes; Rp5.000 remains open). A full request that does not fit yields generic shared `422` on `amount`, even if a smaller valid amount fits. Keep that predicate distinct from over-cap validation; do not disclose remaining capacity/reason. Closed/ineligible remains `409`.
- **R6 — Sandbox authority:** New Donation is persisted `pending`; backend-controlled deterministic scenario/fixture may resolve it to `success` or explicitly demo-labeled `failed`. Caller cannot select terminal state. Pending is never auto-resubmitted and no ETA or real settlement promise is made.
- **R7 — Guest email purpose/lifecycle:** Name/email are excluded from public projections. Email is opt-in, separately verified from Account email, and cannot be used for status/access messaging before ownership verification. Send at most one status-only simulation-labeled notice on terminal success/failed, never pending; follow approved retention, retry, and terminalization limits.
- **R8 — Status credential and privacy:** Issue the selected 256-bit CSPRNG bearer for fragment handoff and client URL cleanup; store only dedicated-purpose HMAC verifier and issue/expiry data; constant-time compare; hard expire at issue + 24h; return status only. No PII, credential, or sensitive request body in ordinary logs.
- **R9 — Uniform status failure/cache behavior:** Absent Donation and missing/wrong/expired credential return byte-equivalent public 404 Problem response status/body/headers/cache policy. Apply `private, no-store` to both success and failure. Deployment-specific abuse, timing and exposure controls must meet their owner/evidence gates.
- **R10 — D1 admission/close ordering:** Campaign owns eligibility and close reason. New Donation acceptance and every applicable close contender produce one order: close-first rejects, admission-first while eligible accepts. Public GET/read-only `published` state is not authorization. Use the Human-selected coordination/lock direction in D13, without silently changing its mechanism.
- **R11 — Atomic settlement and stable close:** Pending→success and full Funding increment commit together exactly once or neither does. Failure, rollback, concurrent success, or replay cannot lose/duplicate increments. Accepted pending Donation settles in full after close without reopening Campaign or changing the first close reason; funding may exceed `max_amount`, but not the finite representability ceiling.
- **R12 — Campaign-owned interface and bounded lifecycle:** Cross-domain calls use an explicit boundary; Campaign owns cap/lifecycle/availability/close reason, Donation owns Donation state/idempotency/contribution. The accepted GET action is advisory; POST rechecks. Do not add scheduler, force-close or public Slice-3 result behavior.
- **R13 — Public sandbox truth:** QRIS is the only active simulated method; other displayed methods remain unavailable/non-interactive. No public guest name/email, real-payment instruction, or implication of provider settlement.
- **R14 — Migrations, errors, SQL and protected paths:** Use parameterized `goqu`, exact decimal types, intentional expected errors and generic safe public Problems. Migrations preserve existing Campaign Funding semantics and accepted defaults; no migration/index is manually applied by an agent. Protected transaction/locking, crypto/auth, and state-machine files are untouched absent exact Human authorization and any paired process required by root policy.
- **R15 — Evidence and acceptance:** Verify every rule independently at the evidence boundary in §12. Contract validation, unit tests, race detector, or generated types alone do not prove database atomicity, anti-enumeration topology, external email fulfillment, or real-world settlement.

## 8. Interface Contract

**Persistence/data shape:** Use exact-decimal monetary values and parameterized `goqu`. Campaign stores the accepted cap with create default, PATCH preservation and publication freeze; existing rows receive default. Campaign Funding remains finite at IDR 99,999,999,999,999,999 whole Rupiah. Admission accounts for settled Funding plus accepted-pending reservations. Donation amount storage is exact decimal and must fit the accepted individual cap; do not invent a universal scale. Request idempotency identity/payload and settlement replay state are separate. Status credential stores verifier and issue/expiry metadata, not bearer. Guest email follows established AES-GCM/HMAC conventions and accepted verification/outbox direction; exact columns and operational bounds must follow applicable owner gates. No network call inside a database transaction.

**API/event/external interface:**

- `POST /campaigns/{campaignId}/donations`: guest; accepted request and shared idempotency header; pending response; QRIS only. Over-cap or eligible capacity no-fit returns shared generic `422 ValidationError` on `amount`, without remaining-capacity/reason disclosure. Closed/ineligible and changed-payload key reuse retain accepted `409` behavior. Resolve a matching prior intent before new-intent closed rejection.
- `GET /donations/{donationId}/status`: optional credential header; status-only success; `private, no-store` on 200 and uniform 404 for absent/missing/wrong/expired. Credential issuance travels in the response for fragment handoff; fragment is not sent to backend.
- Public Campaign detail: required `max_donation_amount: {amount, currency_code}` closed object, amount decimal string and explicit IDR, including Funding-unavailable state. Exact response top-level shape updates from nine to ten fields; no capacity or internal close-reason field is exposed.
- Campaign `donation_action`: accepted WU-S2-005 availability-only contract; informational snapshot only. Donation POST makes the current authoritative eligibility/capacity decision.
- Optional terminal notification: verified recipient only; status-only simulation notice, at most once after terminal success/failed, with durable outbox and idempotent dispatch under approved operations controls.

**Cross-layer/business boundary:** Campaign owns cap configuration, lifecycle, availability, and winning close reason. Donation owns accepted state, retry identity, simulator transition and contribution. A Campaign-owned coordination seam serializes D1 and the full settlement/Funding transaction. No client read projection is authorization.
