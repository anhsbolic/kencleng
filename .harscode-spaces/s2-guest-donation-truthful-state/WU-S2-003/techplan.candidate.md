## 2. Scope

**In scope:**

- Implement guest Donation submission, same-intent retry, persisted pending/terminal simulator states, guest status access, and opted-in guest terminal email according to accepted Slice 2 sources and the gates in §13.
- Add the minimum Campaign-owned D1 admission/close/settlement coordination required by the accepted Campaign and Donation invariants, including exact-once full Funding and capacity reservation.
- Implement the accepted backend `POST /organizations/{organizationId}/campaigns` draft-creation and `PATCH /campaigns/{campaignId}` draft-update contract needed to configure `max_donation_amount`. Implement the complete accepted request/authorization/validation behavior for these two operations (including their other specified fields), with the accepted cap default, omission preservation, and freeze-after-publication behavior. The minimum logical persisted facts are representative membership for an `Owner` or `Staff` user scoped to an Organization, and the Organization `status` plus `has_overdue_report` used by the accepted fresh create check. Keep initial population seeded/operator-assisted; resolve who may establish/update those facts and the governance/control path in §13 before enabling those handlers or any data-update operation.
- Implement the accepted Campaign GET `donation_action` availability projection; it is informational, and submission independently rechecks current eligibility.
- Preserve the already implemented public `max_donation_amount` Campaign domain/read projection and exact ten-key HTTP wire shape from BLD-S2-003-001; integrate it with the accepted detail/action behavior and verify it against the reconciled API. Its prior focused unit/HTTP checks and bounded Review/Testing do not establish migration application or whole-spine correctness.
- Add migrations and focused unit/HTTP plus PostgreSQL-backed evidence needed for the above. Migration/index application remains human-triggered.

**Out of scope (explicit):**

- Changes to `frontend/`, Product/spec/API authority, or the accepted seven-source snapshot; generated/frontend counterparts are already reconciled. A newly discovered contradiction must be routed to its owner.
- Account prerequisite, claim/history, public donor list, guest-email reveal, Campaign-wide notification, real payment/provider rails, or real-world settlement claims.
- Full Slice 3 closure/result lifecycle, deadline scheduler, administrative force-close, or public closed-detail activation.
- Campaign draft `DELETE`, curation, publish/schedule, unpublish, media-write, and other lifecycle operations are outside this Run's newly routed create/PATCH scope. Do not expand the Campaign lifecycle beyond the accepted create/PATCH contract. Do not derive membership from JWT claims, create Organization self-service/representative-management UI, or add an unowned endpoint/operator role for changing eligibility facts. The human-routed minimum persisted source/integration is in scope; the human/product/security authority and governance for setting/updating Organization truth remain open.
- Any write to protected ledger/transaction-locking, disbursement state-machine, crypto, or auth-core paths without the specific Human authorization required by root `AGENTS.md`.
- Any assertion that sandbox tests or email simulation establish external settlement, independent verification, production delivery, or residual-risk acceptance.

## 4. Rules & Validation

- **R1 — Scope and guest path:** Implement only accepted guest submit/status, simulator, required opted-in notification, minimum Campaign D1 integration, accepted availability/cap projection, and the Human-routed Campaign draft create/PATCH scope in R16. Account, donor list/history/claim, real rails, Campaign delete/other lifecycle operations, and broader Slice 3 remain absent.
- **R2 — Exact amount and per-Campaign cap:** Accept whole IDR from Rp5.000 through the effective Campaign cap, including Rp5.001; reject below-minimum, fractional, invalid, or over-cap input. Persist Donation amount as exact integer Rupiah with zero fractional digits; preserve exact decimal wire/calculation behavior without float or rounding. This feature's amount type does not establish other features' scale. Campaign create/PATCH cap configuration is covered by R16.
- **R3 — Public cap wire shape:** Every Campaign response and public detail serialize `max_donation_amount` as exactly `{amount, currency_code}` with required decimal-string amount and IDR. The cap remains present when Funding is unavailable; the public Funding projection keeps its accepted tagged unavailable semantics. Preserve the existing typed DTO/mapping and ten-key exact-wire assertion; whole-spine contract evidence must match the accepted generated contract.
- **R4 — Request idempotency ordering and equivalence:** Under the selected Campaign-first transaction boundary, resolve an existing key and compare the complete accepted request intent before new-intent eligibility rejection. Intent equivalence includes the Campaign route ID and every accepted request field after API defaults are applied: amount, currency, payment method, `event_id`, guest name, guest email, email opt-in, and anonymity. Same key/equivalent intent returns the original Donation after close; changed intent conflicts; a genuinely new key proceeds to current atomic admission. Use the endpoint-wide key scope and keyed HMAC fingerprint over the canonical accepted request, including optional PII; store no raw request JSON and delete the fingerprint with its idempotency record. The bounded record lifetime remains an O3 gate. Settlement replay remains separately idempotent.
- **R5 — Capacity admission and errors:** Require authoritative settled Funding. If unavailable, admit no new Donation and never substitute zero; return generic `503 Problem` without internal reason or `Retry-After`, and resume admission only after the authoritative value is restored. Count collected settled Funding plus full accepted-pending obligations and never admit above IDR 99,999,999,999,999,999. Close with `funding_capacity_reached` if residual capacity is under Rp5.000, including zero (Rp4.999 closes; Rp5.000 remains open). A full request that does not fit yields generic shared `422` on `amount`, even if a smaller valid amount fits. Keep that predicate distinct from over-cap validation; do not disclose remaining capacity/reason. Closed/ineligible remains `409`.
- **R6 — Sandbox authority:** New Donation is persisted `pending`; backend-controlled deterministic scenario/fixture may resolve it to `success` or explicitly demo-labeled `failed`. Caller cannot select terminal state. Pending is never auto-resubmitted and no ETA or real settlement promise is made.
- **R7 — Guest email purpose/lifecycle:** Name/email are excluded from public projections. Email is opt-in, separately verified from Account email, and cannot be used for status/access messaging before ownership verification. Send at most one status-only simulation-labeled notice on terminal success/failed, never pending; follow approved retention, retry, and terminalization limits.
- **R8 — Status credential and privacy:** Use a difficult-to-guess bearer for the settled fragment handoff and client URL cleanup; verify through a one-way HMAC verifier; hard expire at issue + 24h; return status only. The accepted sources do not select a numeric strength/length or credential-generation recipe, key-purpose/provisioning/lifecycle controls, comparison mechanism, or expiry-enforcement mechanism. Resolve and evidence those details through O4 before claiming status-exposure acceptance. No PII, credential, or sensitive request body in ordinary logs.
- **R9 — Uniform status failure/cache behavior:** Absent Donation and missing/wrong/expired credential return byte-equivalent public 404 Problem response status/body/headers/cache policy. Apply `private, no-store` to both success and failure. Deployment-specific abuse, timing and exposure controls must meet their owner/evidence gates.
- **R10 — D1 admission/close ordering:** Campaign owns eligibility and close reason. New Donation acceptance and every applicable close contender produce one order: close-first rejects, admission-first while eligible accepts. Public GET/read-only `published` state is not authorization. Use the Human-selected coordination/lock direction in D9, without silently changing its mechanism.
- **R11 — Atomic settlement and stable close:** Pending→success and full Funding increment commit together exactly once or neither does. Failure, rollback, concurrent success, or replay cannot lose/duplicate increments. Accepted pending Donation settles in full after close without reopening Campaign or changing the first close reason; funding may exceed `max_amount`, but not the finite representability ceiling.
- **R12 — Campaign-owned interface and bounded lifecycle:** Cross-domain calls use an explicit boundary; Campaign owns cap/lifecycle/availability/close reason, Donation owns Donation state/idempotency/contribution. The accepted GET action is advisory; POST rechecks. Do not add scheduler, force-close or public Slice-3 result behavior.
- **R13 — Public sandbox truth:** QRIS is the only active simulated method; other displayed methods remain unavailable/non-interactive. No public guest name/email, real-payment instruction, or implication of provider settlement.
- **R14 — Migrations, errors, SQL and protected paths:** Use parameterized `goqu`, exact decimal types, intentional expected errors and generic safe public Problems. Migrations preserve existing Campaign Funding semantics and accepted defaults; no migration/index is manually applied by an agent. Protected transaction/locking, crypto/auth, and state-machine files are untouched absent exact Human authorization and any paired process required by root policy.
- **R15 — Evidence and acceptance:** Verify every rule independently at the evidence boundary in §12. Contract validation, unit tests, race detector, or generated types alone do not prove database atomicity, anti-enumeration topology, external email fulfillment, or real-world settlement.
- **R16 — Campaign draft create/PATCH:** For `POST /organizations/{organizationId}/campaigns`, require an authenticated `Owner` or `Staff` representative of that Organization, freshly check Organization `status = verified` and `has_overdue_report = false`, validate the accepted create fields, default omitted `max_donation_amount` to IDR 1,000,000,000, and return the effective closed cap object. For `PATCH /campaigns/{campaignId}`, require an authenticated representative scoped to the owning Organization, accept edits only while status is `draft`, validate present fields, preserve cap on omission, and return the effective cap. A supplied cap is whole-IDR IDR in the inclusive Rp5.000–Rp1.000.000.000 range. No cap change is allowed after publication. Use the minimum persisted membership and Organization facts; do not weaken accepted authorization/freshness predicates.
- **R17 — Eligibility truth setup and consumption:** Initial representative and Organization eligibility records may be populated through the MVP-permitted seeded/operator-assisted path only after the authorized source and its governance are identified. Do not introduce an app-facing self-service or representative-management workflow, infer facts from claims, or allow an unowned operator role to assert eligibility. Until authority and controls for setting/updating `verified` and `has_overdue_report` are explicit, do not enable the affected Campaign create/PATCH handlers or any source update operation. This gate does not block unrelated Donation work subject to its own §13 prerequisites.
- **R18 — Minimum D1 schema access and integrity:** Under the approved Campaign-first lock order, pending reservation aggregation filters `donations.campaign_id = locked campaign id AND donations.status = 'pending'`; use a matching partial index on `campaign_id` for pending rows, with `amount` available to the aggregate if supported and justified by the observed query plan. Request lookup uses the endpoint-scoped unique idempotency key before eligibility checks. Persist only `pending|success|failed`, IDR amounts constrained to Rp5.000–Rp1.000.000.000 as exact whole Rupiah, and immutable accepted amount/Campaign/request identity; terminal transitions are conditional on current `pending` state and must affect exactly one row, so terminal Donations cannot transition again. Donation-to-Campaign FK uses `RESTRICT`/`NO ACTION` (never cascade) so seed replacement/deletion cannot erase Donation history. Preserve Campaign→Donation lock order and add no stored reservation counter absent a demonstrated need.
- **R19 — Migration compatibility and rollback posture:** Add minimum D1 schema as an additive successor after migration `000012` (confirm the next migration number at authoring); inspect existing-row violations and table size before constraints/backfill, stage nullable additions/backfills/validation where needed, and assess PostgreSQL-version-specific lock impact before writing SQL. Keep existing Funding values untouched. A pre-write down is schema cleanup only; after Donation writes, down would erase Donation/retry/terminal/guest-data history and added Campaign close/threshold state. Treat that as destructive, guard against accidental post-write down, and use forward correction unless the Human explicitly accepts data loss. No execution, application, reversibility, or lock evidence is claimed.
- **R20 — Campaign close-reason compatibility:** Keep `closed_reason` nullable for legacy `closed` rows whose reason is unknown; do not fabricate history. Every new close winner must persist its accepted reason in the same Campaign-owned transition, and a later contender must neither replace it nor reopen the Campaign. Preserve Donation rows on Campaign deletion/replacement as R18 specifies.

## 8. Interface Contract

**Persistence/data shape:** Use exact-decimal monetary values and parameterized `goqu`. The accepted cap is already stored/projected by the bounded BLD-S2-003-001 change; Campaign create/PATCH still require accepted default, omission-preservation and publication-freeze behavior, and existing rows must keep the `000012` default. The minimum logical eligibility source consists of (a) representative membership scoped to an Organization and user, with the accepted `Owner`/`Staff` level; and (b) Organization `status` plus `has_overdue_report`, read fresh for create. Initial records may be seeded/operator-assisted. Human routing assigns this minimum source/integration to WU003, but authority and controls for setting/updating Organization facts remain unresolved in Open Item 7; do not select physical ownership/schema or create a write path that implies authority before that decision. Campaign Funding remains finite at IDR 99,999,999,999,999,999 whole Rupiah. Admission uses authoritative settled Funding plus persisted accepted-pending reservations; unavailable Funding admits nothing and is never zero. Persist Donation amount as an exact integer-Rupiah value with zero fractional digits, from Rp5.000–Rp1.000.000.000 inclusive. Keep request idempotency separate from settlement replay: endpoint-wide unique request key; compare Campaign ID plus all accepted request fields after defaults with a keyed HMAC fingerprint over the canonical request, including optional PII. Store no raw request JSON, treat the fingerprint as sensitive derived data, and delete it with its bounded idempotency record. The bounded record lifetime remains an O3 gate. Donation state is constrained to `pending|success|failed`, and terminal writes are conditional on pending state. Pending aggregation predicate is `campaign_id = ? AND status = 'pending'`, with a matching partial Campaign index. Donation Campaign FK is restrictive; no cascade can delete Donation history. Campaign close reason stays nullable for legacy closed rows with unknown reason, while every new close winner records its reason atomically and preserves the first winner. Status verification uses the settled one-way HMAC direction; exact stored fields and lifecycle remain subject to O4 resolution. Never persist or log the bearer credential. No network call inside a database transaction.

**API/event/external interface:**

- `POST /campaigns/{campaignId}/donations`: guest; accepted request and shared idempotency header; pending response; QRIS only. Over-cap or eligible capacity no-fit returns shared generic `422 ValidationError` on `amount`, without remaining-capacity/reason disclosure. Closed/ineligible and changed-payload key reuse retain accepted `409` behavior. Resolve a matching prior intent before new-intent closed rejection.
- Funding-unavailable submission: fail closed and return the accepted generic `503 Problem`, with no internal reason and no `Retry-After`; no Donation is admitted. Exact authored API and internal/generated consumer counterparts were accepted in WU-S2-007. This is internal/repository-development compatibility evidence, not external rollout evidence.
- `GET /donations/{donationId}/status`: optional credential header; status-only success; `private, no-store` on 200 and uniform 404 for absent/missing/wrong/expired. Credential issuance travels in the response for fragment handoff; fragment is not sent to backend.
- Public Campaign detail: required `max_donation_amount: {amount, currency_code}` closed object, amount decimal string and explicit IDR, including Funding-unavailable state. Preserve the implemented ten-field top-level exact shape; no capacity or internal close-reason field is exposed.
- Campaign `donation_action`: accepted WU-S2-005 availability-only contract; informational snapshot only. Donation POST makes the current authoritative eligibility/capacity decision.
- Campaign draft writes: `POST /organizations/{organizationId}/campaigns` and `PATCH /campaigns/{campaignId}` follow the accepted `CampaignCreateRequest`/`CampaignUpdateRequest`, representative authorization, draft-only edit, and specified `401`/`403`/`409`/`422` outcomes. Create checks fresh Organization verification/overdue-report state. Cap omission/default/preservation and closed `{amount, currency_code: IDR}` response are exact. No `DELETE` or unrelated Campaign lifecycle endpoint is included. The endpoints and any source-data update path remain disabled until Open Item 7 establishes who may set/update Organization truth and under what governance.
- Optional terminal notification: verified recipient only; status-only simulation notice, at most once after terminal success/failed, with durable outbox and idempotent dispatch under approved operations controls.

**Cross-layer/business boundary:** Campaign owns cap configuration, lifecycle, availability, authoritative Funding read, and winning close reason. Donation owns accepted state, retry identity, simulator transition and contribution. A Campaign-owned coordination seam serializes D1 and the full settlement/Funding transaction. No client read projection is authorization.
