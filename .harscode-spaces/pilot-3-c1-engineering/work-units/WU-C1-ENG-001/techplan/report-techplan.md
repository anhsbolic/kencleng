## Architecture / Plan

The browser signs in through Google OIDC; the backend verifies identity and maps it to a local person and opaque session. The person prepares a named Organization, sees the four consequences, and explicitly confirms. One PostgreSQL transaction checks the session, guard, and same-person preparation, then persists the Organization and initial Owner together. Same-person reads support confirmation replay and later inspection. A closed or unavailable guard prevents a new grant. The five-task plan keeps shared contract, backend identity/session, backend aggregate/guard, operating documentation, and frontend work in scoped tasks. T1's shared contract is complete and reviewed. ## Interface Contract

The contract is an authenticated, same-origin browser API. Mutations require the session, exact Origin, JSON, and session-bound CSRF header. The browser receives no provider token or bearer credential. Owner-scoped reads do not reveal another person's records.

| Concern | Contract |
|---|---|
| Sign-in | `GET /auth/google/start` and `GET /auth/google/callback`; one-time browser-bound state, nonce, and PKCE; successful verification maps a person and rotates the local session. |
| Prepare | `POST /organization-establishments`; session/Origin/CSRF; `{display_name}` creates a preparation only and returns its ID, frozen name, expiry, version, and consequences. |
| Confirm | `POST /organization-establishments/{id}/confirm`; same-person session/Origin/CSRF and `{consequence_version}` only; returns the established Organization after commit or same-result replay. |
| Inspect | Same-person preparation read, `GET /me/organizations`, and Owner-scoped `GET /organizations/{id}`; unknown and outside-person records share a not-found result. |

**Input:** `display_name` is trimmed, 1–200 Unicode scalar values, and rejects controls; duplicate names are allowed. `consequence_version` must match the frozen disclosed preparation version (`c1-v1`).

```json
{"display_name":"Example Organization"}
```

**Output:** A preparation receipt contains ID, display name, expiry, consequence version, and the four disclosed consequences; it creates no Organization/Owner. Established output identifies the durable Organization and Owner relationship, internal authority scope, Organization-provided information source, and establishment effects.

```json
{"state":"established","organization":{"id":"…","display_name":"Example Organization","your_role":"owner","owner_authority_scope":"kencleng_internal","information_source":"organization_provided"}}
```

**Error behavior:** Invalid input does not mutate; unauthenticated and Origin/CSRF-denied requests do not grant authority; unknown/out-of-scope records return 404. Expired uncommitted preparation requires a new preparation. Closed/unreadable guard returns unavailable. Processing failure and uncertain outcome are distinguished; on uncertainty the client reads or replays the same preparation, never blindly creates a replacement. Errors do not expose SQL, provider details, stack traces, paths, or secrets.

## Key decisions

| Decision | Why / consequence |
|---|---|
| One PostgreSQL aggregate transaction creates Organization and initial Owner together. | Prevents either side from appearing successful alone; durable attribution remains inspectable. |
| Google OIDC maps to an immutable local person and opaque local session. | Provider tokens do not become browser authority; email/domain does not grant Owner. |
| Require explicit consequence disclosure and confirmation. | The person sees the Organization, initial Owner, internal-only authority, and information-source consequences before effect. |
| Use a durable conservative guard, closed by default, with restricted operator control. | Holds new grants when permission is absent or unreadable without inventing conflict-matching or adjudication policy. |
| Approved planned baseline: Go 1.26.9, OIDC v3.16.0, OAuth2 v0.34.0, go-jose/v4 v4.1.4, pgx/v5 v5.9.2, and x/text v0.41.0. | Pins address the recorded affected ranges as a plan. Actual graph, source, integrity, scan, compatibility, and compile remain Build gates; no residual risk waiver is recorded. |
| Resolve uncertain confirmation by same-preparation read/replay. | Avoids duplicate grants or blind replacement after a possibly committed request. |
